# Rowan valve supply conditions
Date: 3 October 2026
Owner: Niall Bracken, purchasing officer
Reference: EN-ROW-483

Rowan supplies the small isolation valves used on the workshop's demonstration water loop. The loop operates with clean water during teaching sessions and is drained before the building closes for the winter break. These conditions describe the purchase of replacement valves and the evidence needed when a supplied item fails. They do not cover servicing the lighting installation or equipment borrowed from neighbouring classrooms.

Each delivery must carry a batch label that remains readable after the outer carton is removed. The receiving officer checks the number of valves against the packing note and photographs any crushed packaging before moving the goods into the stores. Cosmetic marks on a carton are recorded separately from damage to a valve. Rowan provides a 16-month warranty on the supplied valves. The period begins when the receiving officer signs the delivery note, not when a tutor first demonstrates the loop.

A claim must identify the batch and describe the conditions under which leakage was observed. Staff should preserve the failed part for inspection instead of attempting to polish its seating surface. Rowan may request a photograph of the connection, but that request does not authorise disposal of the original component. Damage caused by freezing is outside the agreed operating conditions.

The standard order contains fourteen valves and two sets of blank labels. Labels are administrative supplies and have no effect on the warranty period. Deliveries normally arrive at the east loading door before the lunchtime teaching changeover. If that entrance is blocked, the driver waits for the stores contact; leaving a carton beside the public gate does not count as acceptance. A signed count discrepancy is resolved before the next order is released.
