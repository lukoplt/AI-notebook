# Rowan measurement handover
Date: 8 October 2026
Owner: Eva Rowan, measurement lead
Record: HAND-EN-07

The receiving and workshop teams reviewed how measurements from the replacement valve batch will be checked before the components enter teaching stock. The handover concerns the measuring fixture used on the demonstration loop. It is separate from the visual count of delivered goods, which purchasing has already signed. A complete count does not establish that the measuring fixture is ready for use.

Eva Rowan is responsible for calibration. She will compare the fixture against the reference setting, record the observed difference and sign the calibration sheet before the first teaching session. Other staff may prepare the bench and retrieve the reference case, but they must not sign in her place. If Eva is unavailable, the coordinator postpones the check and records the delay rather than treating the previous sheet as a fresh result.

The reference case belongs on shelf four when it is not being used. Its blue handle distinguishes it from the grey case containing spare connection pieces. The workshop assistant will replace a torn inventory label on the blue case; that housekeeping action does not constitute calibration. The team also agreed to keep the sample tray dry so that later observations are not confused by water left from cleaning.

At the end of the check, Eva returns the reference setting to its protected position and notes whether any adjustment was necessary. Purchasing receives a copy of the signed result, while the original stays with the fixture log. A training demonstration may use a photocopy, clearly marked as such. Questions about missing signatures go back to the measurement lead before anyone attempts to reconstruct the result from memory or from a photograph of the bench.
