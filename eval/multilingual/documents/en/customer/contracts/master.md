# Alder workshop lighting service agreement
Date: 2 October 2026
Owner: Mara Fenwick, workshop coordinator
Reference: EN-ALD-482

The Alder workshop is replacing the uneven lighting above its shared assembly benches. The evening repair group needs steady illumination to read component markings, while the morning woodworking class needs clear shadows at the cutting guides. This agreement covers the installed lamps, their local controller and the attendance of a service technician. It does not include the portable lamps belonging to individual instructors.

Alder will report faults through the maintenance register, giving the bench number, the time of observation and whether the problem remains after the ordinary wall switch is used. The service team must respond within 24 hours. A response means a named technician acknowledges the incident and agrees the next diagnostic step; it does not mean that every replacement part will arrive within that period. Reports received during a class are logged immediately, but staff must arrange access before anyone opens a fitting.

The supplier will retain six spare diffusers in its own stores. Alder will keep the access ladder in the locked equipment bay beside the west entrance. Neither arrangement changes the response obligation. Replacement lamps must preserve the approved colour temperature so that instructors can compare finishes consistently across the benches.

Once a month, the coordinator records which fittings have been cleaned and which still show dust beneath the cover. Cleaning takes place after the last booking, with the circuit isolated. Any proposal to move a fitting requires a revised bench drawing and a separate quotation. The existing drawing marks twelve work positions, although only eight are used during evening sessions. Acceptance follows a supervised trial with the normal tools and materials laid out, rather than a photograph of an empty room.
