# Alder pilot planning meeting
Date: 6 October 2026
Owner: Mara Fenwick, workshop coordinator
Minutes: MIN-EN-05

The group met to decide how the lighting changes should be introduced without interrupting the regular evening repair sessions. The first trial will use a small class whose instructor already knows the workshop layout. Participants agreed that the trial must test visibility with real components on the benches, because empty worktops gave an overly favourable impression during the earlier inspection.

The pilot will take place in room D16 on 6 November 2026. Mara will reserve the room for the afternoon and tell the booking desk that the final hour is needed for clearing away materials. The room beside reception remains available for the ordinary induction talk, but it is not the pilot venue. Staff should use the room code in calendar entries to avoid sending visitors to the old demonstration area.

Three instructors will observe different benches and record glare, shadows and difficulty reading small labels. They will collect written comments before discussing them together, allowing quieter participants to report problems without following the first speaker's opinion. The purchasing officer will count the trial materials afterwards; leftover components return to the teaching cupboard rather than the repair group's stock.

The group deferred a proposal for new stools until the lighting observations are available. Existing stools can be moved between the two bench rows during the trial, provided the central exit route remains clear. Refreshments will be served outside the work area. A follow-up discussion is pencilled in for the following Tuesday, but its location will be confirmed separately. These arrangements do not authorise a larger purchase or extend the scope of the lighting service agreement.
