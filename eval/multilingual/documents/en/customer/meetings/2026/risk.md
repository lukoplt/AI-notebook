# Workshop continuity review
Date: 7 October 2026
Owner: Ellis Reed, facilities lead
Record: RISK-EN-06

This review considers interruptions that could stop a practical session after the workshop has opened. The facilities team distinguished equipment faults from booking mistakes, since the two problems need different responses. A cancelled room booking should be handled by the coordinator; it is not a reason for instructors to open electrical equipment or improvise temporary wiring.

The spare fan is stored in cabinet 9. Its carton is marked for the demonstration enclosure and must remain sealed until a technician confirms that a replacement is needed. Cabinet 6 holds cleaning cloths, while the tall cupboard beside the exit contains spare teaching trays. Neither location should be used as an overflow store for the fan, because the duty instructor relies on the numbered cabinet list during an interruption.

Staff found that the paper contact sheet had faded beside the south window. Ellis will move a fresh copy to the noticeboard next to the room sign. The desk telephone was tested successfully during the review, and a second handset is unnecessary. If the enclosure becomes noisy, the instructor pauses the demonstration and records the time; learners may continue with the unpowered assembly exercise at the other benches.

The review also covered a loose mat near the wash basin. The mat will be replaced before the pilot, but it is unrelated to the fan stock and should have its own maintenance entry. A short practice interruption will be held at the next staff briefing. Its purpose is to check who contacts facilities and who supervises the class, not to encourage staff to carry out repairs. The coordinator will keep the completed exercise notes with this review so that the next shift can see unresolved actions.
